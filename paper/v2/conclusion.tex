\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We built a benchmark, an execution-semantics oracle and an accounting scheme for
checking behavioural contracts of JCL batch jobs, and used them to debug and
measure RELIC. On the development corpora, pooled F1 rose from 0.9048 (v1.0)
through 0.9510 (v1.1) to 0.9800 (V2) with no false positives, but a trivial
normalised text rule reaches 0.9978, 104 of the 106 negatives are cosmetic, and
the language-model comparison is selection-biased. The reusable outcome is the
methodology and the diagnostic findings, not an effectiveness claim.
Held-out validation, independent oracle validation and a fair language-model
comparison are \textbf{[PENDING EXPERIMENT]}; until they are done, no claim of
superiority over trivial baselines, of independent validation, or of production
compatibility should be made.
