\section{Threats to Validity}
\label{sec:threats}

\paragraph{Construct validity.}
Ground truth is the simulator's verdict under return-code and abend scenarios.
Under the primary definition every reorder is behaviour-changing because of
abend propagation; with normal-completion semantics only, some reorders are
harmless and RELIC flags them (sensitivity analysis in
\texttt{results/final/metrics.json}). A detector ``flags'' a mutant if it
returns any risk item; explanation quality is not scored. Mutants are
synthetic and not grounded in verified migration-failure data.

\paragraph{Internal validity.}
\emph{Oracle--detector--mutator coupling.} The oracle is code-independent of
RELIC (import-checked), but one team wrote the oracle, the mutator and the
detector; the mutator and oracle share a lexer; both were read against the same
IBM reference. A shared blind spot (same-named steps) was found and fixed during
the evaluation; others may remain.
\emph{Fixes selected on the evaluation data.} Defects D-001 to D-004 were found
in, and scored on, the same three corpora. Only v1.0 is a pre-fix result, and
even its provenance is a legacy-mode code path rather than a release checkout.
\emph{Benchmark negatives.} 104 of 106 negatives are comment or MSGCLASS edits;
there are no benign reorder, split, rename or symbol-change negatives.
\emph{Mutator--detector co-design.} The threshold operator only weakens guards and
the detector only reports weakening; guard strengthening is neither exercised nor
reported.
\emph{Language-model baseline.} One model, one prompt, one sample; 183 invalid
outputs, concentrated in step-reorder and job-split operators; documentation
of the model and provider was inconsistent across repository files (the committed
records state \texttt{openai/gpt-oss-20b} via nvidia).

\paragraph{External validity.}
Three demonstration or teaching corpora; CardDemo dominates; single-file
analysis; 66 evaluable files. No production JCL was available. No claim is made
about production compatibility or other languages. Only three contract types
are implemented.

\paragraph{Conclusion validity.}
Mutants cluster within files. Cluster-bootstrap intervals are reported; they
ignore the selection effect above. Per-operator and secondary-subject cells are
small (e.g.\ OMP course: 10 positives, 25 negatives). No significance tests are
reported.
