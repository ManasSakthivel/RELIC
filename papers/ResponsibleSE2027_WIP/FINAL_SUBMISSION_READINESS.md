## 4. Independent review (fresh reviewer, 2026-11-21)

Verdict: ACCEPT WITH REVISIONS. Main issues, each of which is open:
1. The paper says the LLM run is "not reported as a result", yet it reports its MCCs. The saved matched-subset output also shows the LLM above text B on the 155 answered pairs, with intervals excluding zero on L0–L2. That comparison is omitted. This is selective reporting and must be resolved by the authors: report it with caveats, or remove the LLM numbers entirely.
2. The conclusion says duplicates change the size of the comparison, but dedup (−0.338) is the same as L1 (−0.336). The size change comes from relabelling, not duplicates. Fix the claim.
3. Inference: the percentile cluster bootstrap with 16 clusters is weak evidence, and the cited source argues for refinements. The L3 "not significant" should rest on the exact test alone. The statement that the exact test was "fixed before the L3 result was inspected" needs a dated record or must be removed.
4. The contribution bullet says L3 "not significant", while the bootstrap CI excludes zero. The abstract does not mention L3 at all. Say "not significant under the exact test (p = 0.089)" in both places.
5. "Corrected baseline" (L1) overclaims. It is a relabelling by the authors against a same-group oracle. Rename it "relabelled baseline".
6. "Validated" for 151 pairs is circular: the validation is by the same oracle. Drop the word, or define it. State the circularity of L3 (scored against RELIC's own oracle) in the abstract or in the L3 discussion.
7. Novelty: "no published study was found" needs the search protocol (databases, terms, dates) or should read "we did not find".
8. Uncited claims: the JCL-deployment sentence, and the statistical methods (MCC, exact randomisation, percentile bootstrap, McNemar) have no citation.
9. Responsibility framing is thin: define the sense of responsibility used and connect it to the RSE sources.
10. The robustness file lists "relic_v1_1" and "relic_v2" with identical values, and the paper does not explain how they map. The paper also mentions RELIC V1.0 while saying it does not use the earlier V1.0 evaluation. Clarify.
