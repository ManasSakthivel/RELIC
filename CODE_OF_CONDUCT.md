# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

This project is an open academic research artifact. We are committed to providing
a welcoming and harassment-free environment for everyone, regardless of background,
experience level, or identity.

## Our Standards

**Expected behavior:**
- Respectful, constructive technical discussion
- Honest reporting of bugs, limitations, and results
- Proper attribution of prior work
- Openness to critique and correction

**Unacceptable behavior:**
- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language
- Misrepresentation of research results or methods
- Deliberate sabotage of reproducibility
- Plagiarism or attribution fraud

## Scientific Integrity

Because this is a research platform, we additionally expect:
- No fabrication, falsification, or cherry-picking of results
- Clear disclosure of limitations and negative results
- Reproducibility as a first-class obligation

## Reporting

If you witness unacceptable behavior, please open a GitHub issue or contact
the maintainers directly via the repository contact information.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this code
of conduct, and may block contributors who engage in sustained violations.
