8 · Final Research Position

Based on the evidence currently available, RELIC can defensibly claim:

  • The Behavioral Contract Graph correctly and completely extracts TEMPORAL ordering contracts from JCL job streams (micro-F1=1.000 against an independent oracle, 56 verified contracts, CardDemo test split).
  • Confidence-delta differencing detects contract weakening — a class of modernization risk invisible to structural dependency analysis — as demonstrated by 8 ALTER_ERROR_BRANCH mutations undetectable by Baselines A and E.
  • On a closed-set fault-grounded benchmark of 104 positive mutations, BCG achieves F1=1.000 with zero false positives across 144 semantics-preserving negatives (FPR=0, closed-set property).
  • Available execution traces (2 real GnuCOBOL, 38 JCL-ordering simulations) validate 91.8% of extracted contracts, demonstrating the pipeline works end-to-end and that NOT_OBSERVED ≠ CONTRADICTED.
  • The system is fully reproducible: make reproduce-paper1 deterministically reproduces all five experiments.

RELIC cannot yet defensibly claim:

  • Generalization to systems other than CardDemo (OOD test: F1=0.0 on public COBOL — root cause documented).
  • Independent oracle coverage for RESOURCE and CONTROL_FLOW contracts (Level-3 human annotation deferred).
  • Real z/OS execution evidence (GnuCOBOL only; 38/40 traces are JCL-ordering simulations).
  • Enterprise-scale validity (CardDemo ≈ 30 programs, a demonstration system).
  • Probabilistic interpretation of confidence scores (evidence-strength estimate, not calibrated probability).
